M5Stack Unit C6L · ESP32-C6 · SX1262

MeshCore M5Stack Unit C6L UI

A MeshCore Companion community adaptation built specifically for Unit C6L. Its purpose is to turn the built-in OLED into a useful local status interface without changing the basic Companion role of the device.

What appears on the C6L display?

The project adds several OLED pages with practical device and radio information. Basic status can be checked directly on the unit without opening the phone app.

  • MeshCore C6L UI main page
  • signal and radio information
  • RF Monitor radio readings
  • device and BLE connection information
  • clock and selected RGB status functions

Who is this version for?

For M5Stack Unit C6L users who want to use MeshCore Companion over BLE while also making practical use of the C6L screen and hardware controls.
